## Kauf houses
### K1 Catalog new-build: `Bauphase: Haus in Planung` (Bauträger/Fertighaus house + plot package)
**Tells:** future Baujahr, `Erstbezug`, `Bezugsfrei ab` a bare year (the current year on Planung is not a date: small F deduction + ask for the Bauzeitenplan), house-type captions (`Flair 148…`), a "Franchise-Partner*in" Anbieter. A kw-stamped Objekt-Nr. (`4406-313-kw36-31053`, captions repeating its tail) = a weekly marketing batch. "Keine Grundstücksdaten ohne persönliches Gespräch" = lead-gen, not scam → G ~3,0, and "no reservation fee at the first meeting".
- **The headline is NOT the cost.** Sonstiges discloses Bau-/Erwerbsnebenkosten (#364 +120–150 T). These are usually **inclusive of IS24's Kaufnebenkosten** ("inkl. der von Immoscout oben angegeben Kosten"), so total = `Kaufpreis + X`, never `totalCosts + X`. A round figure "gern im Gespräch" is not stable per Haustyp (T&C "Raumwunder 90": 150 T on #604 vs 100 T on #634), so back out `X − IS24 additionalCosts`, give A as a band, and ask why. **A silent Sonstiges ≠ no extras**: hunt Eigenleistung carve-outs ("Wand- und Bodenbeläge sind Eigenleistung" = schlüsselfertig ≠ bezugsfertig) and add your labelled 40–70 T Baunebenkosten estimate.
- **Is the plot included? Order of attack:**
  0. Grep EVERY TEXT_AREA for the Provisionsbezug ("…enthaltenen Grundstückspreis in Höhe von € 130.000", #386; "Nur auf das Grundstück. Das Haus ist provisionsfrei" = included, #388), "Grundstück … im Kaufpreis enthalten" (#387, also provisionsfrei), "Das Angebot beinhaltet", the house-only Aktionspreis "Haus … ab 184.900€*" (#390), and ScanHaus's Lage template "…hat eine Größe von gut N qm. Die Kosten hierfür betragen X [und sind im Preis enthalten]" (read it to the end; without "enthalten" run the residual, #437). A decoded Telekom address is plot evidence too (ScanHaus Fahrland pipeline: Ketziner Str. 8 / 23 / 133 = #369 / #390 / #426).
  1. The Ausstattung list names "Baugrundstück" (#374 massa).
  2. Lage exclusion "auf einem Baugrundstück **nach Ihrer Wahl** … **zusätzlich**" (#367 allkauf): then `Grundstück ca.` and the address are placeholders, B ~2,0, Garten fails, and A adds m² × local BRW (#367: 488 T → 800–940 T). A Lage naming a real Ortsteil that differs from the geo-tag argues FOR a plot (#381).
  3. An exhaustive Leistungsumfang that never says Baugrundstück → out (#377). A boilerplate Lage is only weak evidence.
  4. **BRW residual** (headline − plot × LOCAL BRW) ÷ house m² vs the brand's house-only band. It has power only for plots ≳600 m² (#709 482 m² was inconclusive). The `PRICE_INFO` percentile is same-direction corroboration only (#423 vs #422).
  - ScanHaus "Grundstücksservice … exklusiv als unser Kunde" is series boilerplate (#435/#444). **When inference decides: a two-row scenario table + one written yes/no as next step 1.** Never average rows that straddle the 40 % blocker (#381 3,7 vs ≤2,0).
- **Brand bands:** ScanHaus house-only 2.000–2.450 EUR/m², all-in with plot 4.100–4.600 (down to ~3.600 for Aktionshaus/bungalows or cheap-land Ortsteile like Gollwitz). allkauf Pro Time house-only ~3.600–3.950 bungalow, ~3.000–3.250 multi-storey. **The all-in band tests the plot, not the Ausbaustufe** (#391 in band yet an Ausbauhaus at 960 EUR/m²). A residual above the band suggests a richer package (LivingHaus #387, primarily Ausbauhaus): score both Ausbau scenarios.
- **Ausbaustufe ladder, worst first:** ScanHaus Ausbauhaus (no Bodenplatte, no Haustechnik; #391) → massa (no Innenausbau, 40–70 T) → allkauf Pro Time (Trockenbau + Estrich labour, 20–40 T) → ScanHaus schlüsselfertig (Beläge only). No Bodenplatte = the Gewährleistung splits at the foundation (G 2,5). "Werksseitig KfW 40" doesn't survive a Haustechnik carve-out.
- **G pluses:** Deutsche Reihenhaus AG "Haus in Bau" (turnkey, plot included, provisionsfrei, MaBV Zahlungsplan → no back-calc, G ~4,0, Stellplatz +~13 T; #420), "Erst BAUEN – dann ZAHLEN" (even if only a gallery tile: get it in writing), the T&C Hausbau-Schutzbrief. The Festpreisgarantie varies (12/14/15 months), so compare it with the Bezug. **G minuses:** two contracts (einheitliches Vertragswerk GrESt), Realteilung pending, a title counter **"(1/2)"** = competing house types on one parcel (#604: find the sibling, one enquiry), the Lage tail ("teilerschlossen; weitere Erschließung durch den Käufer", "Realteilung erforderlich", "Gemeinsame Zufahrt"; #386), Generationenhaus "eine von zwei Wohnungen" (#426: the Lage plot figure is the FULL two-unit plot; WEG). A Makler selling a Bauträger package may never name the builder (#386), so ask for the builder + § 650m Bürgschaft.
- **Photos:** renders get the Neubau exception (no cap) but list "keine Fotos des Objekts". Filter logo/seal/slogan tiles (#383, #387). A different house type in the captions (`Flair_110_*` for 118 m², #388) → D down. Two EG plans (4 vs 5 Zi) = the room count may be an upgrade. **A plot-photo caption naming another place** ("Blick aufs Grundstück Michendo[rf]", #604) = zero object evidence + 1 Medium (still Legitimate).
- **Fields:** "Bodenplatte"/`obj_cellar: n` = no Keller. An "Echtholztreppe" in a bungalow = boilerplate. `obj_barrierFree: n` beats the prose. **`Fußbodenheizung` is distribution, not a heat source** (D down, ask). `Etagenzahl: 2` can be a knee-wall DG (two m² per room on the plan). Plans show the Badewanne and blue "bauseits". A Verbrauchsausweis on Planung is impossible (planning value). `funds=` in the finance tile assumes ~20 % equity, so test it against the profile.
- **Dupes and geo:** TDOT relistings (`TDOT NN`, "(1/3)") = dupes when the Telekom street matches (#436 = #390). Adjacent IDs + identical plot/price ≠ dupe: the Telekom house NUMBER decides (#445 Hauptstr. 14 vs #437 17; the 199.000 € Marquardt plot figure is a template). **Exposé:plot is not 1:1** (#364/#395 both Eschenweg 13 = mutually exclusive; #388 = 11a), so key on the address and rank twins by the photo audit. **Read Lage sentence 1 on every "Fahrland"-tagged ScanHaus ad** (#424 = Gollwitz, 30 km; #435 = pasted Brandenburg-Marquardt text). **Border PLZ: PLZ + texts beat the whole geo hierarchy** (#651: all geo fields said Groß Glienicke, 14089 + texts = Berlin-Kladow). A false boilerplate Lage (#377 schools in Uetz-Paaren) gets no B credit.
- **Budget-fit vs overpricing:** in pricier acceptable areas (Michendorf, Falkensee) an €/m²-cap miss at a marktnah price is a search-parameter artefact. The blocker keys on the ABSOLUTE price (#497 large Falkensee plot fires; #501 small one at/below BRW, A ~3,6). **Makler-Sammelinserat** ("Auf Anfrage", placeholder 1.000 m², many Gemeinden, one "Beispielgrundstück"; #497 EMA): a teaser, so ask for a concrete parcel + price + address.

### K2 Bestand Kauf — fields and traps
- **"Kaufpreis: Auf Anfrage"** = `adTargetingParameters.obj_purchasePrice == "0"` (and `price=0` in the financing URLs). It is not a parse failure and not EXPIRED. **These bypass IS24's price filter**, so they show up in any Hauskauf scan (#380: a Griebnitzsee villa in a ≤500 T search). It doesn't imply a sparse ad (#384: 60 photos, plans, EA), so score D/H on what's there. **Still give a budget verdict:** first one WebSearch on the address (the price is often public on another channel). Otherwise bound it (plot m² × BRW as a hard floor; #380 lake plot ≈ 4,5–7,5 Mio floor), label it your estimate, and don't formally fire the blocker on an inferred number ("would fire on disclosure"). Never contact just to learn it.
- **Maklervertrag traps:** Sonstiges or the `Provisionshinweis:` itself can claim the exposé request forms a Maklervertrag, or make one "in Textform" a precondition for price/documents/viewing (#384 Paegel). Read the full Provisionshinweis. Check that the § 656c BGB hälftige Teilung is confirmed (usually not): a G deduction + ask for it in writing, and ask for the price without confirming the Provisionshinweis.
- **Wohnfläche can be Wohn- + Nutzfläche summed** (#366: 160 = 117 + 43,94 Remise). Cross-check TOP_ATTRIBUTES vs `Nutzfläche ca.` and the text. `Zimmer` on an entkernt house is a planning figure. The IS24 attribute can count the kitchen (#590: plan "5-ZIMMER", attribute 6).
- **`Etagenzahl`** can contradict the Grundriss captions (#372 "1" but EG/OG/KG plans = split-level). Count floors from the plans.
- **Energieausweis:** `adTargetingParameters.obj_energyEfficiencyClass` ("H") is a text field (plus `obj_energyType` `energy_required` = Bedarfsausweis). The ATTRIBUTE_LIST shows it only as an `IMAGE` url `…/energy-efficiency-labels/H.png`. The kWh value is often absent from the API, so check the EA PDF. **"laut Gesetz nicht erforderlich"** is legally correct only WITH a `Denkmalschutzobjekt:` CHECK (§ 79 GEG): then it's no scam signal and no D omission, but Denkmal is a G minus (genehmigungspflichtige Sanierung) and an A plus (§§ 7i/10f EStG AfA). **Without** the CHECK on a non-Denkmal house >50 m² it's simply wrong (§ 80 Abs. 3 GEG vorlagepflichtig; #372): a real gap, G deduction, ask for it. **Rentals:** Denkmal often only in title/text, and the string → score the Potsdam Mietspiegel on the **`kein EA` row** (operative) and quote A/B as the landlord's best case (#582). Energy claims are then unbelegt, and Denkmal + Gas + Innendämmung is a D negative (GEG 65 % → § 559 risk, mould). **EA row missing entirely and no Denkmal word** (#638 Bj 1849, Nauener Vorstadt): check Baujahr + address against the Denkmalliste/Denkmalbereich. If plausible, it's no scam signal, still use the `kein EA` row, and add "Denkmaleigenschaft nachweisen" to next steps.
- **Financeability:** compute `(equity − Kaufnebenkosten) ÷ Kaufpreis`. IS24's `funds=` assumes far more equity (#376: 85 k vs 50 k → ~97 % Beleihung). The binding constraint is financeability, not the headline.
- **Sanierungsbedürftig + class G/H:** apply the Bauträger extra-cost doctrine: score A on Kaufpreis + Sanierung (K5).

### K3 "You can't move in" family (owner-occupier profile)
- **`{"type":"CHECK","label":"Vermietet:"}`** in Hauptkriterien = sold tenanted (§ 566 BGB). An Eigenbedarfskündigung per unit (3–9 months + § 574 + Räumungsklage) → **F ~2,0, G ~2,5**. "Eigenbedarf kann angemeldet werden" is no bezugsfrei guarantee. `Mieteinnahmen pro Monat:` may be in the Kosten list (#373). Check whether it's Kalt or gross. Genuinely absent while the Lage sells "Kapitalanlage" = a real gap and question 1 (#394). A low priceBar position (~0,14) is explained by the tenancy, not a scam. Tenanted exposés often have zero interiors plus logo padding.
- **`Bezugsfrei ab: "spätestens {date}"`** = occupied now without the CHECK (#376). Score F on the date, but demand a Räumungszusicherung in the Kaufvertrag (without it F ~2,0).
- **Nießbrauch / Leibrente** (in TITLE, Sonstiges "WAS BEDEUTET NIESSBRAUCH?", priceBar ~0,06; #370): Abt. II first rank, ends at death (convert the stated age to a horizon; plural owners = the longer life), no use AND no rent, bank financing "in der Regel nicht möglich" (the finance tile is inapplicable), "Eigentümer tragen alle Kosten" is overstated (§ 1041 = only gewöhnliche Unterhaltung). → **F 1,0, G ~1,5, cap 2,0.** Watch the structured vs text price mismatch and a Provision "auf den Marktwert". Openly disclosed → Legitimate.
- **Verkauf gegen lebenslanges Wohnrecht / "Kauf in die Zukunft"** (#460: Marktwert 630 T, price after Wohnrecht 330 T) → F 1,0, G ~2,0, **cap 2,0**: "only relevant as a Leibrenten investment". **Hidden variant** (#378): three bullets at the END of the Ausstattung list ("eingetragenes Wohnrecht … Vormerkung … Ausgleichszahlung 333.333 € bei Freimachung"). **Read Ausstattung to its last line on every housebuy.** No Eigenbedarf against a dingliches Recht → F 1,0, G ~1,5. Score A on Kaufpreis + Ausgleichszahlung + NK (#378 → +78,7 % → blocker). "Eingetragene Vormerkung" (§ 883) = a third party's claim is already secured. Ask whether the Ausgleichszahlung triggers GrESt again.

### K4 Kauf photos, WEG, portfolios
- **Caption filter before counting:** drop `type:"AD"`, logo tiles (caption == company name), bare place/landmark names (`Werder`, `Stadtschloss`, `Barberini`, "Haus kaufen-Potsdam…"), Visualisierung/render captions, and **caption-only placeholders** ("Grundriss gerne auf Anfrage", #380). Existing house + zero interiors → D cap 3,0.
- **"Digital bearbeitete Fotos" for tenant privacy** = real rooms retouched → −0,25, no cap, exculpatory. **Mirror: "Bilder des MUSTERHAUSES"** (tail sentence, #394 THE GROUNDS) = blanket, covering even object-looking captions → **cap D 3,0**. **Pixel watermark "KI-bearbeitet"/"KI-möbliert"** (#590), nowhere in the JSON → open 2–3 images when D hinges on photos. KI-möbliert = the pictured EBK isn't confirmed.
- **THE GROUNDS Real Estate Development AG (Fahrland) portfolio sell-off:** Objekt-Nr. `{PLZ}-{NN}` (`14476-19`, `14476-35`) + AGENTS_INFO "Team Fahrland-Werder" = a developer unloading a stock unit by unit. Sister units likely exist, some maybe **bezugsfrei**, so on a tenanted dead end, ask for a vacant baugleiche unit. The EA PDF + object-specific Grundriss sheet give the street address, the real room count and which house in the row. Sloppy boilerplate → H 3,0, and check the seller's Bonität.
- **WEG-aufgeteiltes Reihenhaus:** often only one description sentence ("Die Fläche des WEG-Grundstückes beträgt 2.658 m²", #376). `Grundstück ca.` can be the unit's share (#376 165) OR the **WEG total** (#590 2.067, #373 819). Grep `WEG|Gemeinschaftseigentum|Teilungserklärung|Sondernutzung|Gartenanteil` on every Reihenhaus. Consequences: Garten/Carport only via Sondernutzungsrecht of unstated size; a shared Gas-Zentralheizung means the GEG swap is a majority vote; undisclosed Hausgeld/Rücklage/Teilungserklärung/Beschlusssammlung = G 3,0 and an undisclosed A cost. When given (#373 Wohngeld 458 + Rücklage 60), compute net yield/carrying cost.

### K5 Existing house vs catalog, Sanierung arithmetic
- **Discriminator first:** `obj_constructionPhase: no_information` + `obj_newlyConst: n` + a past Baujahr + room-name captions = a real existing house. The headline IS the price and `FINANCE_COSTS.totalCosts` is trustworthy. Don't run the plot ladder (#393).
- **`obj_condition: no_information` + "Potenzial"/"solide gebaut" prose = unsaniert** (D ~2,5 ± EA). **`Baujahr laut Energieausweis`** = the last substantial measure and usually the boiler: equal to Baujahr → no energetic modernisation; later (#392 1958/1995) → the Ölkessel is ~30 y. **§ 72 GEG Betriebsverbot** (the owner-occupier exemption ends at Eigentümerwechsel, 2 years) → § 71 65 % EE → envelope first. **§ 47 GEG Nachrüstpflicht** (oberste Geschossdecke, pipes) within 2 years. Pre-1995 Gas/Öl on Kauf in Potsdam (Wärmeplanung passed) → a Wärmepumpe 25–40 T → G ~3,5, and ask the boiler age first. Öltank: Stilllegung 2–5 T + Altlastenkataster. Pre-1978: Schadstoffgutachten.
- **`Letzte Modernisierung: {year}`** = the most recent single measure, however trivial (#375 "2025" = one Dachfenster). Score D off the dated Ausstattung list.
- **Sanierungsbedürftig + G/H → A on Kaufpreis + NK + Sanierung** (your labelled estimate, Kernsanierung ~1.600–2.500 EUR/m² pre-1960; #382 → +23–43 %, #392). Don't fire the blocker on an inferred number. **The doctrine cuts both ways** (#385 at 170 T: all-in 19–35 % UNDER budget, ~79 % Beleihung). Run the order: (1) all-in, (2) all-in €/m² vs `max_price_per_m2` (breaches earlier), with a second row converting Ausbaureserve/Nutzfläche, (3) equity − NK. **The Provision is what flips it** (#392 3,57 % → 97 % Beleihung). Show a two-row table + a #385-vs-this comparison. D bottoms at 1,0 but a strong A/C/E can carry ~3,8. **KfW 261/BEG is next step 1** (the Tilgungszuschuss is the decisive open number). A historic Siedlung named in Lage (#382 "Am Stadtrand" 1932–36) → check Ensembleschutz/Erhaltungssatzung.
- **Land-residual sanity check:** plot m² × BRW vs price. A low priceBar position (0,09–0,35) explained by land value + Sanierungsstau is not "far below market".
- **Keller:** a CHECK present = confirmed. CHECK absent proves nothing. `obj_cellar: n` is weak, and **a Grundriss caption naming a basement level overrules it** ("unverbindlicher Grundriss Kell", Gartengeschoss; #673). Check the Etagen enumeration.
- **Structured vs text contradictions** (Zimmer 6 vs "fünf Zimmer" #382; plot 930 vs "approx. 963" #393): report the structured value, flag the delta, and ask for the Katasterauszug. `obj_barrierFree: n` beats an elevator claim.
- **`obj_lotArea == obj_usableArea == Grundriss Brutto-Gesamtfläche`** = the Anbieter pasted the gross building area into the plot field (#708 252,44 vs real 216). Distrust the plot.
- **Neubau/Townhouse Wohnfläche can be ~20 % outdoor** (#708: Terrassen @33–50 %, Dachterrasse @50 % → 39,88 of 205,90 m²). Read the Grundriss PDF per-floor table and score C on the enclosed area (quote both €/m²). The EA PDF names the real Gebäudetyp ("Einfamilienreihenmittelhaus" vs "Townhouse").
- **"Auf Anfrage": WebSearch the address FIRST** (street + Nr + m² + Ort). A co-lister often publishes the price (#708 Kensington KBT-403 875 T, earlier 895 T). Match on address + Wohnfläche + plot, **fire the blocker on a published price**, and flag the channel risk (the co-lister may charge Provision: "only via the IS24 Anbieter"). WebFetch/curl get 403 on kensington and atlas, so read the WebSearch summary itself. **Then check sibling units of the same seller:** DB Immobilien (Marko Fensch) Objekt-Nr. `43090103-10348xx` = consecutive units of one terrace (#378 Eckhaus …829 at 500 T + Wohnrecht, #898 Mittelhaus …830 Auf Anfrage, identical m²/plot/EA); grep `data/listings.md` for the Objekt-Nr. prefix and identical m² + plot. A sibling's disclosed price (+ Ausgleichszahlung) bounds the Auf-Anfrage unit, so the blocker can fire on it. Otherwise bound it: `obj_lotArea` × Ortsteil BRW (average and top value) + `obj_livingSpace` × NHK 2.200–2.800. If land at the average BRW already eats the cap, the blocker is proven (#673 Groß Glienicke Seevilla). Quote the €/m² axis separately. Not a scam signal. **Filter hole:** `obj_purchasePrice: 0` passes every price cap, so the size cap is the cheap substitute filter.
- **KI per-image captions on Kauf** (`Inspiration N (KI-Generiert)`, often `.png` vs `.jpg`): bucket real / KI / Grundriss / ad tiles ("kostenlose Wertermittlung"). No cap, −0,5, and name which subjects are rendered (#673: the Außenanlage/lake frontage the price rested on). "Virtual Staging" with the unstaged original of the same room present = no deduction (#392). Staged-only room = −0,25, also when the staging is unlabelled (#898 DB Immobilien "Wohnzimmer"/"Schlafzimmer" = staged copies of empty rooms elsewhere in the gallery; only a `montage` shows it). The #651 counter-rung and #740 middle rung are in §Photos of [[immobilienscout24]].
- **Transparency credits in H** (#740 allod): Sanierungsbedarf in sentence 1, staging labelled, Weitwinkel disclosed (measure rooms on site), EA PDF, 360°, 2D+3D plans, Keller/Heizraum photos. Score D on condition and H on disclosure. **Contentless marketing prose is an H deduction**, and on such ads never-mentioned amenities count as absent (#392).
- **Buyer Provision is legal only with the mirror clause** (§ 656c/d): ask for written confirmation. English/German twin exposés by the same agent = one object (contact once).
- **Anchors:** Bornim ~400–500 EUR/m² BRW (my estimate); **Bornim is a preferred Ortsteil (3–4 km from Golm)**, so B 4,5+ once the text and tag agree. Uetz-Paaren/nördliche Ortsteile ~200–300. A rural Lage that honestly says "in den umliegenden Ortsteilen" is credible (B ~3,5). Don't apply #377's false-claim deduction to every village. `obj_telekomInternetAvailable: false` is a real B con.
- **`obj_condition: ripe_for_demolition` ("Abbruchreif") on a housebuy = the product is a PLOT** (#707 Sternstr. 45 Drewitz). Wohnfläche/`Kaufpreis €/m²` are book values. A = Kaufpreis + NK + Abriss (30–60 T, asbestos pushes it up) + a new build (400–550 T for 130–150 m²) → 0,9–1,1 Mio vs "2 % under cap". D 1,0, E −0,5 (Keller/Stellplatz belong to the demolition object), F = 18–24 months, not "sofort". G carries claimed-only Baurecht, Abrissgenehmigung (Dorfkern Denkmal/Erhaltung), Erschließung (#707 only electricity confirmed), Altlasten (Öltank). A low priceBar percentile above `minSimilarPrice` means no scam signal. Captions from different years (`20240623_…` + `20260625_…`) = long marketing time, which is a lever.
- **Address leak no. 4: the title block of old building plans** in "Weitere Dokumente" (Schwarzpausen, `pdftotext` = 0 bytes → `pdftoppm -jpeg -r 100 -f 1 -l 1` + Read): "Fam. Horn, Drewitz, Sternstr. 45" (#707), plus the original heating type (an Altlasten hint). The other leaks are Telekom base64, MEDIA/Grundriss captions (`90864 Objekt Hege16 EG` → treat as a hint and ask, #706), and the Grundriss PDF header.
- **Deutsche Bank Immobilien "digitaler Maklervertrag" gating** (#476: address/extra photos/viewing only after confirming a free Maklervertrag, MAP address null): live and scoreable. The Telekom leak gives the address, and it is not a scam signal. Next step: confirm the free contract.

## Plots (`realEstateType: livingbuysite`)
Skeleton: TOP_ATTRIBUTES = Kaufpreis + Grundstücksfläche. The Hauptkriterien are Vermarktungsart / Grundstück ca. / Erschließung / Empfohlene Nutzung / Verfügbar ab. Key `adTargetingParameters`: `obj_buildingPerm`, `obj_development`, `obj_shortTermBuild`, `obj_constAfter`, `obj_demolition`, `obj_immotype`, `obj_lotArea`. BRW anchors: [[bodenrichtwert-brandenburg]]. Provision on a Kauf is legal (Bestellerprinzip is rentals only).

### P1 Bauträger-/Hausbindung — grep the whole JSON; the phrasing varies
Grep **all TEXT_AREAs (Sonstiges, Lage, Objektbeschreibung)** for `Bauträgerbindung|Baubindung|Hausbindung|wird bebaut mit|Bauverpflichtung|Bauvorhaben werden durchgeführt von|massa|Town & Country|Danwood|Scanhaus|Hanse Haus|{Hausmarke}|@{hausmarke}.de`. ⚠ **`Baupartner` is a false positive** (#643 "mit Freunden oder Baupartnern zusammen" = co-buying). Count only a hit that names a company or imposes an obligation.
Rungs (Block G):
- **Waiver in the text** ("kann auch separat, ohne Hausvertrag, erworben werden"; Hanse Haus rep Margit Dossmann/Dossmann Immobilien, #075/#602) → **G ~3,0**. Get the option into the notarial contract. A fully verified funnel (TNS + Gold Partner + complete Impressum) → H ~3,5, Legitimate. The same rep runs both Provision models, so never infer tie-in strength from the Provision field.
- **One sentence in Sonstiges** ("Das Grundstück wird bebaut mit einem massa Haus nach Wunsch", #528), with real price, full parcel, `Provision für Käufer: Nein` → G ~2,5. **0 % Provision on a plot is a tell** (the seller earns on the house), though still a plus in A.
- **Explicit "Es besteht Bauträgerbindung"** (#483 ROSTOW) → G ~2,5. Cheap €/m² is the hook for the build contract. The effective total ≠ plot price.
- **Title denies it** ("Grundstück zur freien Bebauung", #592 FIBAV) while Lage+Sonstiges carry it → G 2,0. Never trust a "frei/bauträgerfrei" title before the grep. Seller and tied builder can be one group under near-identical names (FIBAV Immobilien / FIBAV Massivhaus Immobilien). Call out the commission paid to the tied party.
- **"N. Baufamilie gesucht" / "Grundstücksanteil ab X EUR pro Familie"**, price "Auf Anfrage" (#519 massa haus rep): the headline area is the WHOLE DH plot. **Divide by families before any €/m² check** (850 m²/2 → 259 not 129 EUR/m²). "ab" + "Auf Anfrage" → A ≤2,5, G ~2,0 (tie-in + unknown second family + undivided plot).
- **Confirmed free** (zero grep hits + Makler-GmbH with HRB/§ 34c + `obj_courtage: y`, #546) → a Block-G PLUS: free builder choice and no einheitliches Vertragswerk.

### P4 Plot fields: what each really says
- **Use class first:** `Empfohlene Nutzung: Mehrfamilienhaus` / `obj_recommendUtil: apartment_building` = a developer parcel, the fastest disqualifier (#399). If the field is absent, grep the TITLE for `Bauträger|Investor|Anleger|Projektentwickl|Firmen` (#402) and the text for `BGF|Boardinghouse|Hotel|Büro|Gewerbe` (#428). Score C on the profile's intent. `Einfamilienhaus` / `twinhouse:single_family_house` + a published price = the matching combo.
- **"Auf Anfrage" plots often state the price or BRW in prose:** Lage `Bodenrichtwert` (#401 900 EUR/m²), Sonstiges/Objektbeschreibung `€|beträgt|ab \d` (#428 2,8 Mio; #456 "ab 280.000"). A disclosed price fires the blocker. A developer seller's achieved flat prices → residual value (#401).
- **`obj_constAfter`:** `constructionplan` (B-Plan, often with `obj_GRZ`/`obj_GFZ`) > `neighbourconstruction` (§ 34) > `no_information` > `externalarea` (§ 35 = not buildable). `obj_recommendUtil: no_development` → E 1,0 in one field (#535). **The dropdown loses to the text**: a named B-Plan in the Objektbeschreibung (#400 Nr. 36-3) or a positive Bauvoranfrage (#402; ask its date, ~3-year validity). Grep `Bebauungsplan|B-Plan|Geltungsbereich|Baufeld|Baufenster|Satzung`. An unnamed "FNP und B-Plan liegen vor" next to "§ 35" is noise (#535).
- **Baurecht ladder, best first:** B-Plan in attribute + text (#406) > B-Plan in text only (#400) > an expired granted Baugenehmigung (#534, § 72 BbgBO 3 years; E ~3,5, fetch the Bauakte) > Klarstellungs-/Ergänzungssatzung (#543: settles *where*, not *how much*; E ~4,0; check the Geltungsbereich covers the whole Flurstück) ≈ positive Bauvoranfrage (#402) > Ortsgestaltungssatzung with caps (#405: fields at the floor, text solid, E ~3,5; convert the 100 m² Grundfläche cap into Wohnfläche) > § 34 "unverbindliche Auskunft" (#404 E ~2,5) > "**denkbar**" (#529) > silence (#403 E ~2,0). A demolished predecessor + built neighbours = factual § 34 (#545 E ~2,5). `obj_buildingPerm: n` always caps E. Pull GRZ/Baugrenzen yourself. Mitigation: a Bauvorbescheid § 74 BbgBO as a purchase condition.
- **`obj_shortTermBuild`:** `n` is the Baurecht floor only when `constAfter` is empty too. It can just reflect an unvermessene parcel (#534) or carelessness beside a real B-Plan (#406). `y` is not Baurecht either (#404). **The #529 quartet** (`shortTermBuild n`, `buildingPerm n`, `constAfter`/`development` `no_information`) + "denkbar" + an open-field aerial → E ≈ 2,5, and the rear half may be § 35. An existing Wochenendhäuschen + a § 34 story can be a Sondergebiet (#447).
- **GFZ/GRZ turn m² into buildable m²** (#406 GFZ 0,20 × 1.000 = 200 m² GF). Surplus buys garden. **GRZ ≈ GFZ ≤ 0,10 = Wochenendgebiet** (#517).
- **Geometry is C:** grep `\d+ ?m ?x ?\d+ ?m|Straßenfront|Front von ca`. 13 m frontage → ~5–7 m Baufenster (#403); 26 m is fine (#405). "Optional auch mit X m² erwerbbar" = the boundary isn't fixed and that price is unnamed (#406). **`Fläche teilbar ab:`** is a size claim, not a legal one: each half must einfügen, and it's a C plus only if both clear `min_m2`. Its absence on a sibling = lost optionality, not a C penalty (#543/#544).
- **Map images beat the prose** (captions `Flurlage|Teilstück|Teilungsentwurf|Lageplan|Flurstück|Vermessung`): Flurstück, amtliche Fläche, the neighbours' buildings (direct § 34 evidence). **Identify plots by Flurstück, not house number** (#543 "Erikaweg 14" = Flurstück 186; siblings need not be adjacent). A shoelace on the polygon validates the dimensions. **Maps + Gütesiegel tiles can make `obj_picturecount` 100 % fictitious** (#529: 0 real → D cap), but they locate address-less plots. An image captioned like the Objekt-Nr. is often the LGB-Luftbild with borders (#535: Handtuch strips in a ploughed field → C 2,0). A `B-Plan-Uebersicht…FNP` image is a transparency plus.
- **Same Makler, two live IDs for one plot:** MD5 the image bytes, not the URLs (#490/#529). Evaluate anyway with a near-dup table, H −0,5, no scam signal (same price). Parcels listed 1 m² apart dodge IS24's dup filter.
- **Defects live in the Objektbeschreibung** (#399: Wasserschutzzone II vs III, UNESCO buffer, a public Geh-/Fahrrecht, a Miteigentumsanteil at a neighbour; "u.a." = more). Speicherstadt: `Unterbaurecht` for the city, a WEG Tiefgarage next door (#400). Read the Lage tail too.
- **Liabilities:** a retained Fundament (Altlasten/Kampfmittel). `Abriss:` CHECK = built on → 15–30 T + DDR asbestos + 3–6 months, and who pays? (#403). **`obj_demolition: n` can lie** (#545 "zu 90 % abgerissen" + Bauschutt; #517 "Abrissbedarf"): grep `abgerissen|Abriss|Rückbau|Teilunterkellerung|Bauschutt` and look (D ~2,0). A genuine "keine Bäume, kein Abriss" is a D credit (#404). Trees are a liability, and position matters (#405 front = the house spot; Baumschutzsatzung, § 1a Ausgleich). Demolishing a grandfathered Bestand kills Bestandsschutz (#517).
- **Hinterlieger / 2. Baureihe** (#534 "SICHERUNG ZUFAHRT ÜBER BAULAST"): a Baulast is public-law only, so ALSO demand a Grunddienstbarkeit Geh-/Fahr-/Leitungsrecht. Hausanschlüsse over 40–90 m → 15–35 T. § 34 Hinterlandbebauung is the case that fails.
- **LSG/Wald share on ordinary plots:** grep `Landschaftsschutz|LSG|Naturschutz|Außenbereich|Wald|Erholungsfläche|Waldumwandlung` and redo €/m² on the buildable part (#534: 76 gross → 214 on ~700 m²). "inkl. Waldumwandlungsgebühren" (#543) = Wald under § 2 LWaldG despite the Satzung.
- **Plot Provision isn't under §§ 656c/d:** the buyer may bear it all. 3,57 % is a plus (#404), 5,95 % Falkensteg, 7,14 % #406 (quantify the saving at the norm). A voluntary 50/50 (#405) = G plus. Stated payment terms = a seriousness tell.
- **Scam/transparency on plots:** admissions against interest are exculpatory. Above BRW = clean. **Below BRW = a classification, not a bargain** (BRW = baureif; #529). With a disclosed restriction explaining it + verified Makler it stays Legitimate (#405). "Unterlagen erst bei Gespräch mit Kaufabsicht" + no price = Medium. - **Brandenburg items no field carries:** the Baulastenverzeichnis ≠ Grundbuch, Kampfmittel-Verdachtsland, einheitliches Vertragswerk GrESt. Inner Potsdam: gemeindliches Vorkaufsrecht + Gestaltung (ask the Stadtplanungsamt). - **Check the area against EVERY search in profile.yml** (#405 Beelitz is in the Freizeit search's areas): quantify so the user decides the config. Ortsteil ≠ Gemeinde (#404 Grünefeld). A Berlin-facing commute ≠ Potsdam-facing.
