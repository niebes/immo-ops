## §Dating — how old is the ad?
- **The mobile API has no `onlineSince`.** Date the ad with these surrogates and cross-read them. **A low Scout-ID is a recycled ad object, not a stale listing.**
  0. **Best: the orte address page** (recipe in [[immobilienscout24]] §Access) embeds every live exposé at that address as JSON with `"@creation"`, `"@publishDate"` and `"@modification"`. Grep `expose/{id}\",\"@id\"` and read the next ~300 chars → the exact publication timestamp (#907: rental 171547976 @publishDate 2026-10-06). It lists the address's Kauf AND Miete ads side by side, so the same sweep finds a parallel sale ([[immobilienscout24]] §Anbieter). *Why:* before it, ads were dated from media-ID and Scout-ID ladders (± weeks).
  1. `contact.freemiumSettings.dateStarted/dateEnding` = (re)publication day + the contact deadline for private ads.
  2. Camera-original captions (`20151208_111118`) date the photos.
  3. `obj_cId` = the account (low = long-standing; also the private re-list key, §Identity).
  4. Date references inside the text, and `Bezugsfrei ab`.
  5. **Upload suffix in `fullImageUrl`/PDF URLs** (`…/{uuid}-{n}.jpg`). A `1[0-9]{9}` value that decodes to the past is an epoch. **Sanity-check it:** future dates (#591, 2030) or consecutive integers `2xxxxxxxxx` (#588) = a sequential media-ID. A plausible past date can still be bogus (#773: "2018 04:01" on summer daylight photos of a 2021 building), so check season/time/Baujahr. The media-ID is monotonic, so calibrate it: ~2.087–2.090 Mrd on 2026-09-12, ~0,65 M/day. Finished evals' JSONs under `tmp/eval/*/` are free calibration points (never a running sibling's dir). Captions `"testfilename"` are an upload-tool artefact, not fraud.
  6. **Scout-ID ladder from the repo's own reports** (max ID per report month): 05/26 168,06 M · 06/26 168,94 M · 07/26 169,66 M · 08/26 170,31 M · 12.09.26 170,71 M · 06.10.26 171,55 M (#907) ⇒ ~0,75 M IDs/month. Regenerate rather than trusting these numbers. An independent anchor for a low ID: the Kleinanzeigen twin's poster "Aktiv seit" (#887: ID 161,9 M ≈ autumn 2025, KA account since 29.09.2025 → private owner re-letting after ~1 year = a turnover question for H, not a stale ad). Plots sit far longer than flats, so a low ID on a `livingbuysite` is normal.
  7. Season cues in the photos (autumn foliage in an August ad = ≥9 months old, #588) → "Fotos nicht tagesaktuell" ✗ + ask for current photos.
  8. PDF `CreationDate` (`pdfinfo`) of attached plans/EA ([[immobilienscout24]] §Docs).
- **Legacy IDs (6x,xxx,xxx ≈ 2012) are usually re-activated by the same private owner at each turnover** (#750: active, `dateStarted` 2 days old, future Bezug, newer media-IDs for the interior shots). Never EXPIRED on the ID alone. `dateEnding` then = "anfragen HEUTE".
- **Old ID + old photos** (#507, 2015 exposé recycled by the private landlord, `dateStarted` today) → live. D −0,5 for stale evidence, 1 Medium scam signal (decade-old photos), H notes turnover/Eigenbedarf. **Old ID + current media-IDs** (#680) → refreshed gallery, no D penalty and no Medium. Two media batches inside one gallery date its expansion. Ad age is a negotiating lever either way; ask "seit wann inseriert?".
- **Dating can refute a claim:** `obj_lastRefurbish` is landlord-editable and gets refreshed without new work (#582: "Stand 2026", but photos from 03/2022 and a Grundriss PDF CreationDate of 05/2021). § 556f (umfassende Modernisierung) exempts only the FIRST letting afterwards, so cross-date before accepting it, and put § 556g Abs. 3 Auskunft in next steps. **"kernsaniert" with no year:** a Makler portfolio page often dates the first letting (#875: nudelmann-friends.de/portfolio "Gutenbergstr. 94–95, … 8 WE, Januar 2020, Ø 17 €/m²") — WebSearch `"{Straße} {Nr}" {Ort}`. It decides first letting (§ 556f) vs re-let under the Bremse and gives a Vormiete anchor.

## §Identity — duplicates, re-lists, twins
- **Verify the hint first.** "Row #NNN has the same title" is sometimes plain wrong (#755 vs #009). Grep the tracker row and the old report H1 before any curl. Say in the report which field refuted it. A landlord-cluster table of the scored siblings (m², Kalt, €/m², Etage, differing amenity, score, 404 status) beats a bare dup check.
- **Rent + m² is no identity key in a flat-rate estate.** Kalt ÷ m² landing on a round figure means the whole house is priced off one table (talyo Drewitz "Tower" 13,00 on every unit; Vonovia Kirchsteigfeld ~10,3–10,6). Decide on **`obj_floor`, Aufzug, EBK, Freisitz type, NK structure, Anbieter, Objekt-Nr.** *Why:* #576 was routed as a swap's landlord channel on 820≈822 EUR and was a different flat. **But not every estate is flat-rate**: BUWOG Brunnenallee spans 13,73–16,70 in one 2018 quarter. There €/m² IS a discriminator, a 10 % cheaper unit is a real bargain (check priceBar + an exact 3,00× Kaution), and the Mietspiegel verdict must be re-derived per unit (#758).
- **Objekt-Nr. (`OBJECT_INFO`) taxonomy:**
  - Landlord unit key: Vonovia `82-…`/`90-…` (digits before the trailing counter = the building), `Stein124 WE32-1`, `H2-02-09` (Haus/Etage/Whg). Identical = same unit. A suffix added between postings (`263119-36`) is a re-post counter.
  - Makler `{prefix}#{hash}`: the hash is noise. A semantic prefix (`p-luis-5.58` = Haus 5 WE 58) is a unit key. An opaque prefix means matching on address + m² + rooms + amenity set + floor.
  - `{9-digit}#{hash}` in the scoutId range (`166780518#38Exe`, #681) = the predecessor scoutId. Curl it: 404 = re-list (old price unknown, so no price-delta signal), 200 = two live ads.
  - TENANT_NETWORK UUID = per ad, identifies nothing.
  - **ohne-makler cross-posts: Objekt-Nr. = the bare OM id** (#876: 465357 ↔ `ohne-makler.net/immobilie/465357/`). Curl the OM page ([[ohne-makler-net]]) for fields IS24 drops, e.g. "Denkmalschutzobjekt: Ja", which decides the Mietspiegel row when the EA says "laut Gesetz nicht erforderlich".
  - **`obj_objectnumber == obj_scoutId` = private, no key.** In a same-address cluster, diffing `obj_objectnumber` + `obj_floor` + `obj_privateOffer` separates the landlord channel from the Nachmieter channel (the CLAUDE.md dual-listing call; #698 EG vs #699 1. OG were 2 flats, identical to the euro).
- **Private poster identity: `adTargetingParameters.obj_cId` (+`obj_cwId`) = the IS24 account.** Same cId = same poster, and it rules out a hijacked copy. A tenant often re-posts as a normal private ad (initials, new title), whose Kalt/NK fields may be placeholders with the real Vormiete only in prose (#794 = #739). #770 vs #760: same cId, the re-post stripped all photos, changed Hausnr 6 → 4 and added Index + 24 M Mindestmietdauer. Diff the text and the picturecount, not just the price.
- **Same cId, same address, one Kauf + one Miete exposé = the same flat for rent AND for sale** (#907). Not a dup to fold and not a "reposted with different prices" signal. Scoring and next steps: [[immobilienscout24]] §Anbieter.
- **Tenant-ad identity:** `AGENTS_INFO.name` + `geo_ot` + rooms + ~m² + **`obj_totalRent` to the cent**. Title, text and photos are regenerated on each re-post, but one shared photo settles it. Same warm rent ⇒ byte-identical bands, which is arithmetic, not a dup (#739 vs #561).
- **Cross-portal:** a captioned Grundriss image ("1. OG · Whg. 1.07 · ca. 71,50 m²") beats every number (#699). **Normalise the rent type first**: KA private ads quote warm, IS24 `obj_baseRent` is kalt (#639 vs #313: "+50 EUR" was warm 1.700 → 2.200, which refutes the match). A shared landmark (BUGA = Volkspark) is zero evidence. Objekt-Nr. and old-ID 404 tests only work IS24↔IS24. **A KA twin of a private IS24 ad can quote IS24 Gesamtmiete + Stellplatz as "Preis"** (#887: KA 2.000 = 1.970 warm + 30 Außenstellplatz) while KA `nebenkosten_i` held the KALT (1.600) and `warmmiete_i`/`kaution_i` matched IS24 to the euro: reconcile add-ons before calling it a "reposted with different prices" Medium.
- **IS24 twin of a scored Kleinanzeigen flat = the price-split resolver** (#761 vs #694): identity via verbatim text + Kaution/Ablöse to the euro. Re-score only the blocks whose facts changed (section 0 in the report). Trust the IS24 split and confirm Kaution ÷ 3 = Kalt. A still-live KA ad = parallel reach expansion. The price gap is a noted-and-refuted Medium and contact Q1.
- **Confirmed re-list (same key + old ID 404):** grep `reports/` for the ADDRESS to get the whole chain (#337 → #617 → #677). Quote the step and cumulative delta plus vacancy: that is the lever (Mietbeginn, Kaution, Stellplatz even at an unchanged price). Keep block scores aligned unless a field moved or a newer rule applies (say "corrects #NNN"; #677 dropped exactly to `max_price_per_m2` → A 4,0 → 4,5). Two disagreeing predecessors (#442 3,6 vs #465 4,0 on identical data): follow the better-evidenced one per block. F decays as the same date nears. Same advertiser + price DOWN → no "different prices" signal (also for a private re-poster, #621). **Zero-delta re-list after months** (#742 vs #116) = the market rejected the ask. Negotiate Mietbeginn, not price. Re-read the new text (it can reveal the street, #743), and carry photo findings forward with their source.
  - **Photo identity across a re-list: never by `md5sum`.** IS24 re-uploads the gallery with new media IDs AND re-encoded bytes/resolution, often reordered (#900 vs #842: 9/9 same frames, 0/9 md5 hits). Compare `montage` contact sheets of both sets side by side. The fastest whole-payload proof is a key-by-key diff of `adTargetingParameters` (#900: only `obj_scoutId`/`obj_objectnumber` differed, same `obj_cId`). *Why:* the md5 test alone called identical photos "different" and weakened the re-list call. (Kleinanzeigen differs: there byte-identical files are expected, [[kleinanzeigen-de]] §Poster.)
- **Validate `contact.phoneNumbers[]` structurally** (#742: 13 digits, Ostfriesland area code, button hidden = IS24 garbage). It is an H oddity, never a scam signal. The Festnetz/mobile label is unreliable.
- **privat → Makler re-list: Kalt DOWN, NK UP, warm flat = Mietpreisbremse evasion check** (#626 vs #495: 1.223/300 → 893/600, the NK now 6,38 EUR/m² vs a ~3,00–3,80 norm). Diff Kalt/NK/warm separately → A −0,5, and next steps are § 556g Abs. 3 Vormiete + the Betriebskostenabrechnung. The Makler ad has better data and worse photos, so carry the private ad's facts forward as questions.
- **Sibling landlord ad at the same address** (#698 Semmelhaack vs private #699): its cost split verifies the private ad to the euro (exculpatory). **Arbitrate private checkboxes against it** (#699 `obj_lift: y`, #698 `n`) and **harvest Baujahr/EA from it** before writing "unbekannt" (it decides the Mietspiegel row and § 556f).
