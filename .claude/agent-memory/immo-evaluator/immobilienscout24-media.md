## §Photos — captions, Grundriss, Block-D cap, amenity evidence
**Core rule: count real photos of the INTERIOR OF THIS UNIT, decided from the pixels, not from captions or `obj_picturecount`.**
- **Download:** `MEDIA[].fullImageUrl` (skip `type: "AD"`), swap `/format/webp/…` → `format/jpg`, curl into the work dir, Read (`dwebp` as fallback). ⚠ The shell is **zsh**: unquoted `$var`/`set -- $p` loops don't split and save error bodies, so wrap loops in `bash -c` (#795). `…png/ORIG/resize` can be a 25–45 MB PNG: PIL-thumbnail it before Read (full-res phone PNGs argue for authentic self-shot photos). For plans never use `legacy_thumbnail/800x600` (crops, #838); use `…/ORIG/resize/1200x1600%3E/format/jpg/quality/80`. `obj_picturecount` counts slots, so dedupe by bytes.
- **Phone-original captions = real, no cap:** `YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS`, a 30-digit id, `image.jpg`, `IMG_####`, an iOS UUID, `WhatsApp Image YYYY-MM-DD at 0`, `IMG-YYYYMMDD-WA0000` (also hyphen-less `IMG{YYYYMMDD}WA{####}`, #1046 — don't anchor the regex on the hyphens), Samsung `YYYY-MM-DDHH.MM.SS…`, a bare number. Dated shapes timestamp the shot: months old = the flat may be empty (lever, no Möbelübernahme; #734); an old date shows an Altvertrag (#817).
- **…but a phone-shaped caption often hides a GRUNDRISS** (#735, #739, #741, #746, #568). Real photos are then 0 → D cap (2,5 with nothing else known). The plan is the **best amenity evidence**: printed "Loggia"/"Balkon" overturns `obj_balcony: n`; it shows a drawn tub, a separate kitchen, a Durchgangszimmer, a windowless Bad. An in-unit "AB" is not a Keller. A plan dates the layout (WBS-70 → 1971–1990; CAD + Loggia + 2 Bäder → post-2000). Plans move E, never D. Other plan captions: `OG`/`DG`/`EG`/`KG` (#680), `Bildschirmfoto …` (#772), a raw CAD filename (`FF26888_…`, #769). **AI-furnished plans without dimensions** ("Präsentiert durch Performer CRM", #861) give room count/layout, not m², and are no D-cap reason.
- **Grundriss captioned with ANOTHER unit's number** ("Grundriss WE4", handwritten "EG WE 04" on a WE 8 / 1. Etage ad, #1047) = the sibling's plan, uploaded with an old photo batch. Sum its rooms against the advertised m² (78,89 + ~45 % Balkon = 82,35 → likely the same stacked layout), say "sibling plan" in C, and ask for the unit's own plan. *Why:* otherwise it is taken as this unit's plan.
- **Real photo ≠ unit photo:** `BEISPIELFOTO` captions (#753), street/foyer-only `IMG_####`, or **upload fillers** (colour gradient, a desk keyboard; #855) → the zero-photo case, not a scam signal (no property shown). **Genuine zero** (`MEDIA` absent or `[]`) + no Baujahr/condition/EA/plan → **D 2,5** (the cap is a ceiling; #621, #635).
- **Promotional banner frames can carry rent terms:** a `type: PICTURE` (not `AD`) stock `.png` with a seasonal caption (#999 ZBVV "Halloween 5": "kaltmietfreier Zeit"), absent from every TEXT_AREA and the Kosten list. 0 unit photos (no D effect), but an A/F lever + contact question (how many months, the asterisk's conditions, an attached Kündigungsverzicht?). *Why:* a caption skim reads it as a filler and loses the only mention of free rent months.
- **Single exterior shot** → base −1,0, no full cap (#525 3,5 vs twin #231 4,5). Read it for the Baualtersklasse: Pfosten-Riegel alu facade, galvanised Gitterrost stair, Edelstahl-Netzgeländer = post-1990; young staked trees = quarter <10 y (§ 556f). **Old masonry + modern inserts** = gutted conversion → the conversion-year row (#738, 1,5× swing). Facade evidence both ways: balconies everywhere → E 2,5 "likely"; Laubengang decks → a negative prior, said in prose (#675). **A single interior shot** can prove a negative (#619 Pantry-Kochnische), date the building (#556) or confirm a must-have (#553): base from the frame, −1,0 for 1-of-N rooms.
- **Photo-rich ads: still open the odd-one-out caption** (#568: `IMG_5784` was the plan, a Balkon visible through the window). "No photo captioned X" ≠ X absent. Judge from the newest capture set.
- **Screenshots:** `.png` + ~0,46 portrait + black letterbox = the tenant's own screenshot rotated 90° (#618), not a scam tell. **Gallery photographed off a screen** (bezel, cursor; #802) = not the lister's images → Medium + H 1,5 + "Proceed with Caution" + in-person viewing first, no D cap (bait underprices; #802 sat at the top of its band). **Phone screenshots of ANOTHER app's gallery** (iOS status bar, caption + "7 / 10" counter + close-X, same clock time; #935) = a tenant re-sharing the landlord's move-in gallery: real unit photos, no D cap, no scam tell (esp. when the same `obj_cId` used the frames before), but current state unverified; name the missing rooms (#935: 4 of 10, no Küche). *Why:* reading it as #802 would have fired Medium + H 1,5 on a legitimate Nachmieter ad.
- **Numeric sort-index captions** (`0`, `1`, `99999` = append-last) are not renders. On Vonovia the trailing `99999` items are usually banners (#815: 7 = 5 real + 2 ads) → `montage` contact sheet. Numeric-ID captions on a *Kauf Planung* listing (`1555066-131917-1-g`) ARE catalog renders: the class decides.
- **ONE example-photo regex over all TEXT_AREA text + all captions:** `Muster|Beispiel|z\.\s?B\.|Referenz|Symbol|Visualisierung|ähnlich|tapezierfertig|unrenoviert` + stems `Musterfoto|Vergleichswohnung|baugleich|typengleich|Beispielwohnung` (captions truncate at **30 chars**: use `Visualisierun|Musterwo|Digital-Home|Renderin|Symbolb|KI-generier`). **Grade by share:** a disclaimer sentence or a mostly-example gallery → D cap 3,0 on existing flats (#564 talyo; #748 "Musterfotos einer Vergleichswohnung" under plain `IMG_6006…` captions). One labelled room among ≥5 real → no cap (#842). Vonovia puts "ACHTUNG: … Musterbilder" / "nur Musterfotos hochgeladen" at the **end** of the Objektbeschreibung (#566/#576/#586/#678/#679/#837) with "tapezierfertig … unrenoviert" (→ ~4–7 T own cost). Openly disclosed = exculpatory; say it was refuted.
- **Existing unit in a young Neubau project with a Musterwohnung gallery → cap** (the Neubau exception is for first letting only): #537 Luisenpark (`backbone_…_WEB` + `{YYMMDD}_{bautraeger}_…-WE` fingerprints, D 5,0 → 3,0); #587 K&D `Musterfoto_{Raum}` with the disclaimer burned into the pixels. **Check house numbers in "real" exteriors** ("Hausansicht GB317" on a Nr. 313 ad). **Zweitbezug + a "Prospekt" PDF (§Docs) → diff the gallery against it** (`pdftoppm -r 45` + `montage` both): #917/#918 (Esplanade, Bj 2016) showed only the 2016 Prospekt's staged Musterwohnung frames under plain captions ("EBK", "Bad"), no disclaimer, so the regex missed it → cap 3,0. *Why:* D would have been 5,0 on 10-year-old brochure shots.
- **Unbuilt project, one media set for N units** (#628: 63 pictures + Matterport, all Musterwohnung/Visualisierung): no cap, but "no photo/plan of this unit" as a ✗ and show sibling €/m².
- **AI staging — decided by caption prefix, disclaimer verb and scope word; never a scam signal** (DAHLER, Von Poll, locals Real Estate do it routinely):
  - A minority of per-image `KI-generiert` labels among real photos → no cap, score from the real frames (#603 DAHLER 14 + 4 KI; #800 Von Poll 12 + 10), ✗ con "renders show the renovated target state" (esp. `need_of_renovation`). "Virtual Staging" with the unstaged original present → no deduction (#392). Staged-only room → −0,25.
  - **Generic "Einzelne Bilder … bearbeitet/optimiert/virtuell möbliert" boilerplate** + unprefixed captions → −0,5, no cap (#740, now on most Verwalter ads).
  - **`^KI-Visualisierung – {Raum}` on most captions + a disclaimer saying `renoviert|entrümpelt|aufbereitet`** → the condition is synthetic → cap 3,0, checking room by room for originals (#651).
  - **Burned-in "AI MODIFIED" pill badge** (bottom right of every image, #884 Nudelmann & Friends 12/12, no text disclaimer): empty rooms whose windows/steps match the Grundriss = AI enhancement (sky/light), not staging → like the generic boilerplate (−0,5, no cap). Only a `montage` sheet reveals it. Kauf twin ("KI-bearbeitet" watermark): [[immobilienscout24-kauf]] K4.
  - **No badge: a `.png` original among `.jpeg` slots marks the edited frame** (`fullImageUrl` `…-{id}.png/ORIG/…`; #953 Nudelmann & Friends, 11 JPEGs + 2 PNGs: the only "Bad / WC" frame was virtually staged with no tub/shower/WC in shot, no disclaimer). Read the PNG frames first. Only when PNGs are the MINORITY: if most slots are PNG with white side margins, they are re-uploaded screenshots of the lister's own photos (#989: 5 of 8, consistent with the plan) — judge by content consistency, don't dock D. A staged frame that is a room's only photo = room unseen → D −0,25 + "real Bad photos?" as a contact question. *Why:* the montage alone read the Bad as photographed while the text claimed Dusche + Badewanne that no photo showed.

## §Docs — attached documents (`REFERENCE_LIST "Weitere Dokumente"` / "Weitere Links")
PDFs sit on `d2qfnj9mv71tll.cloudfront.net/…pdf` (plain `curl -sL`, no auth). **`md5sum` them first**: two differently named entries can be the same file (#755: "RHS_30_MWS_9_…" looked like a unit plan and was the EA). `pdftotext -layout` for vector PDFs. If it returns only a label (`WE38`), the plan is an image: **Read the PDF with `pages: 1`** or `pdftoppm -png -r 110`. **Read plans as images even when the text works**: text gives names, the rendering gives topology (Durchgangszimmer, no Balkon; #582). Image-only PDF → `pdftoppm -r 70 -f 1 -l 1` + Read.
- **Energieausweis PDF:** class + Kennwert when the ATTRIBUTE_LIST says only "liegt vor" (keeps D's EA adjustment neutral, the missing-EA signal Low), plus Gebäudetyp, number of units, Baujahr.
  - **On Altbau/Denkmal it OVERRIDES `obj_yearConstructed`**, which can hold the Sanierungsjahr (#751: ad "2018", PDF "1890 / Sanierung 2017" → bis-1948 row: +46,5 % over vs compliant). Curl it whenever the text (generalsaniert, Denkmal, Stuck) contradicts a Neubau Baujahr. Objective "Sanierung auf Neubaustandard" test = page 2 of a Bedarfsausweis: Primärenergie and HT' Ist vs Anforderung.
  - **Read page 1 even when class + value are in the ATTRIBUTE_LIST:** "Wesentliche Energieträger" + "Baujahr Wärmeerzeuger" can refute `obj_heatingType` (#906: ad "Fernwärme", EA "Gas/Gas, 1998" → HK risk + GEG Modernisierungsumlage), and "Adresse" can be a house-number RANGE that dates the building and gives the WE count (#906: ad 19c, EA "Hauptstr. 19d-f, 17 WE").
  - **The PDF can be an OLDER, EXPIRED certificate than the ad fields** (#1048 K&D Großbeerenstr. 305: fields "Verbrauchsausweis D 108,5, Baujahr 2021"; PDF = Neubau-Bedarfsausweis B 61,3 of 09.03.2016, "gültig bis 09.03.2026", Baujahr 2016 = Bauantrag year). Check "Gültig bis" on page 1; score D on the ad's current figures, make the expired PDF a Low signal + "aktuellen Ausweis anfordern", and don't let its Baujahr move the Mietspiegel row. (Contrast the Altbau override above: there the PDF is the better evidence.) *Why:* B/2016 would have given an EEK bonus and the 2013–2020 field.
  - The **REFERENCE_LIST label itself can leak the address** (`regEnergieausweisPotsdamerStr1`). On Kauf it names the real Gebäudetyp ("Einfamilienreihenmittelhaus" vs ad "Townhouse", #708). A "Vorschau" EA on a Neubau gives Bauantrag vs completion year.
- **Unit Grundriss PDF** (labelled "WE 58"): confirms rooms/area/floor, often with a "Planmaße" disclaimer. **Sum the rooms against the advertised m²** to prove it's this unit (#565). **The headline Zimmerzahl can count the Küche** (#565: 2 Aufenthaltsräume + Küche; grep `Schlafnische|Schlafempore|Kochnische|für Singles`) → C on the real rooms. Compute the Flur+Bad share. Per-floor tables on Kauf/Neubau can include **~20 % outdoor area** (#708 Terrasse @33 %): score the net.
  - **The title block names the programme:** Kirsch & Drechsler "WOHNANLAGE FÜR ALTERSGERECHTES WOHNEN" (#583/#587) → possible Zielgruppen-/Förderbindung (G ~4,0 open question on freifinanziert). "Eigentumswohnanlage" = condo units, Vermieter may be a private investor (Eigenbedarf conceivable). Grep `altersgerecht|betreutes Wohnen|seniorengerecht|Servicewohnen|gefördert|WBS|Belegungsbindung`.
  - **The plan header is an address leak** (street + "Wohnung 3 · 1. OG rechts") **and its title bar can name the OWNER, not just the Verwalter** (#917/#918: "Steuerberaterversorgung Niedersachsen" above a JNP letting, confirmed by the Prospekt; AGENTS_INFO showed only JNP). Institutional owner (Versorgungswerk/KdöR, rental stock, no WEG) ⇒ no Eigenbedarf, H ~4,5.
  - Unit keys in the footer/filename (`WHG-Nr. 0606/47/03001`, `esplanade_G6_OG3_47_03001` = Gebäude/OG/WE) settle sibling dedup. An **embedded Lageplan** names surrounding streets/building letters → the Quartier → grep sibling reports (#759 → Brunnen Viertel).
- **Anbieter's own Selbstauskunft form**: apply on THEIR form.
- **The ad's own earlier version** (`Anzeige_2012-07-31`, #750) = a free rent history (KM/NK/Heiz trajectory; a moderate rise is exculpatory, a big Heizkosten rise is thermal evidence), the landlord's real identity, and field drift exposing stale checkboxes.
- **PDF `CreationDate` (`pdfinfo`) is a dating surrogate** (#565 current letting on an old ID; #582 plan from 2021 refuting "Modernisierung 2026"; [[immobilienscout24-history]] §Dating).
- **No "Weitere Dokumente" on a Bauträger pre-letting** (Objekt-Nr. like `H1-00-02`, Bezug a year out): read `"Weitere Links"` for the project landing page (Baubeschreibung, EA, unit plans), the deepimmo Lagekarte and the Matterport Musterwohnung (#630). No final EA can exist yet → the signal is Low/benign.
- **The Anbieter's own website can publish what the exposé withholds.** Private seller's domain named in Sonstiges (#633 havelgrundstueck.de: Flurstück, survey plan, "VB", interior photos, the seller's identity ~300 km away). Makler: `"Weitere Links"` → "Zur Website …" (KIRSCH & KIRSCH Potsdam, `kirschundkirsch.de/immobilien/{slug}-{Objekt-Nr}`, plain `curl -L` + Firefox UA = 200) lists "Objektunterlagen" PDFs on `live-files.ynfinite.de/v1/file/…pdf` (no login): Grundriss, WoFlV-Berechnung, EA, image-only (`pdftoppm` + Read). Their title blocks gave street + Hausnr. (#962/#963, `obj_street: no_information`, no Telekom leak; the filename suffix `…_4_b.pdf` already was the Hausnr.), the WoFlV schedule the real Zimmerzahl, the real Objektart (#962 "Zweifamilienhaus" → Realteilung or WEG?), and EA "Baujahr Wärmeerzeuger 1995" refuted the text's "Gasheizung 2020" (#963). *Why:* IS24 left both addresses unrecoverable; the website gave them in one fetch.
